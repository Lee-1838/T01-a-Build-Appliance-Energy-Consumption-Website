Welcome to the Appliance Energy Consumption website. This site provides information about how household appliances consume energy in the Australian market.

Understanding appliance energy consumption helps households reduce electricity bills and lower carbon emissions. In Australia, appliances account for a significant portion of household energy use.

Why It Matters

  • Energy-efficient appliances can reduce household bills.
  • Australia has energy rating labels to help consumers choose.
  • Standby power can account for up to 10% of household electricity.

Televisions

Televisions are one of the most common household appliances. Modern TVs vary widely in energy consumption depending on screen size, technology (LED, OLED, QLED), and usage patterns.

Key Facts

  • Average TV energy consumption: 100–300 kWh per year.
  • Larger screens typically consume more energy.
  • Standby mode still uses power — turn off at the wall to save.
  • Australia's Energy Rating Label helps compare models.
